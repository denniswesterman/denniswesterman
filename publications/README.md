The executive paper and its application example explain what the organization must arrange, without prescribing a technical file structure. They are public drafts, not evidence of validated software or achieved savings. The original technical architecture paper remains available separately as supporting detail.

## Repository convention

- English is the primary publication language and profile entry point.
- Language editions use identical, language-neutral slugs under `publications/en/` and `publications/nl/`. The UCF executive paper and example originated in Dutch; their English editions are translations.
- Each paper links directly to its language counterpart and the matching profile overview.
- Shared visual assets are stored once under [`assets/`](../assets/).
